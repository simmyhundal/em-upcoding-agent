# P0114: P0114 (Family Practice, GA): 99215 share is elevated at every complexity level, but the sampled records mostly support the billed 99215s

**Leaning (advisory):** Pattern consistent with a high-acuity panel

## Why

P0114 billed 46 of 239 claims (19.2%) as 99215. Given patient complexity, about 3.7% would be expected. The provider's 99215 share is above the all-provider share in every complexity stratum, including patients with 0 conditions (10.5% vs 2.1%) and 1 condition (11.8% vs 3.4%). On its own, that billing pattern would raise concern. The documentation review points the other way. Of 16 documented 99215 claims, only 1 (C0055570, documented at moderate MDM and 34 minutes) did not support the billed level. That is an unsupported rate of 6.2%, against 24.2% for all providers. Several 99215s on low-complexity patients were documented as high MDM with more than 45 minutes, for example C0055466 (obesity only) and C0055591 (osteoarthritis). The 99214 unsupported rate (10.6% vs 8.4%) is only slightly above the all-provider rate. Examples include C0055406 and C0055588, both documented at low MDM. Overall, the evidence is consistent with a panel whose visits are legitimately more complex than chronic-condition counts suggest, not with systematic 99215 upcoding. Two caveats apply. Only 16 of 46 99215s were reviewed. Undocumented 99215s on 0-condition patients with acute-only diagnoses, such as C0055395 and C0055533 (J06.9) and C0055400 and C0055495 (R10.9), have not been verified.
