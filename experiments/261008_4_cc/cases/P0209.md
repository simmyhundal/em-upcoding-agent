# P0209: P0209 (Family Practice, GA): 99215 share well above what patient complexity predicts, but documentation of 99215 claims is too thin to judge

**Leaning (advisory):** Inconclusive

## Why

P0209 billed 23 of 205 claims (11.2%) as 99215, against an expected 1.7% given patient complexity. The peer z-score is modest (0.87), and the provider was flagged on the risk-adjusted score (3.77). The excess is not explained by a sicker panel. All 23 99215s fall on patients with 0-2 chronic conditions, and none are on patients with 4 or more. In every complexity stratum the provider's 99215 share is roughly 3-5 times the all-provider rate (0 conditions: 9.6% vs 2.1%; 1 condition: 10.4% vs 3.4%; 2 conditions: 21.7% vs 5.6%). The sampled 99215s include routine presentations: URI (C0093991, C0094085), UTI (C0094003, C0094010), abdominal pain (C0093999, C0094122), rash (C0094074, C0094133) and back pain (C0094103). That is a pattern consistent with upcoding. The records review neither confirms nor rules this out. Only 3 billed 99215 claims were documented. One was unsupported (C0094003, documented moderate MDM, 34 minutes), and two supported high MDM (C0094009, C0094161). That gives 33% unsupported vs 24% for all providers, but a sample of 3 is too thin to judge. Documented 99213/99214 claims show unsupported rates at or below the all-provider rate (0% and 6.1%). The claim lists are random samples, and clinical notes were not reviewed here.

- Compare the 99214 share on low-complexity patients with peers, since the mix leans heavily to 99214 (106 of 205).
