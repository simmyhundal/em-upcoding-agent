The 99213 unsupported rate is somewhat elevated (13.1% vs 7.5%, n=61), but that concerns under-level codes and is a small sample. Overall the pattern is more consistent with a legitimately complex panel than with upcoding. The remaining within-stratum excess in the 5+ group is worth a light check. Records cover only 28.8% of claims, and the claim lists are samples.
