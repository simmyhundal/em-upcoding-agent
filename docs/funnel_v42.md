## Reading it
The two largest losses in the level review are the shortlist cap (of 91 true upcoders only 32 reach the shortlist of 40) and the request recommendation (half of the shortlisted providers are not asked), followed by the break-even rule (13 called, 8 demanded). Shortlisting more costs triage and chart review on providers who are mostly not upcoders; that trade is what the list-size experiments measured. The volume screen loses providers only where the office stays silent. Of the 8 level demands, all 8 are true upcoders: the review is precise and has low reach.
