## Decision rule (issue 118)

A design beats (a) in a release when its mean per-seed difference exceeds the seed-to-seed spread (sd) of that difference. The rule asks for this on at least two of the three agent releases A3.3, A3.4 and A3.5.

- Design (b): beats (a) in 0 of 3 releases (A3.3 -3,439 against sd 1,033, A3.4 -2,059 against sd 2,498, A3.5 -2,621 against sd 1,061).
- Design (c): beats (a) in 0 of 3 releases (A3.3 +0 against sd 0, A3.4 +11 against sd 33, A3.5 +63 against sd 129).
- Design (d): beats (a) in 0 of 3 releases (A3.3 -3,439 against sd 1,033, A3.4 -2,070 against sd 2,495, A3.5 -2,684 against sd 1,038).

**Result:** no design passes the rule, so the advice stays advisory in agent v5.0 and the effort moves elsewhere.

Caveats: the final call is approximate (rules decide here); release 2 to 4 numbers favor (b) to (d) because the policy was fitted on those seeds; ten seeds per release with about eight upcoders each; the agent's own first sample can differ from the standard first sample used for the advice.

## What the agents did against the advice

- Almost every departure is the agent **stopping where the policy says escalate** (STOP taken, TARG or AUD advised). The agents stop by not continuing, so these omissions never appear as a logged action: the 79% 'followed' figure of the release 5 advice log counted logged actions only, and overstated adherence per provider.
- Obeying the escalation advice (designs b and d) finds more upcoders (for A3.5, 40 against 31 over ten seeds) but with about 60% more rounds, and each find recovers little under score 4.0, so the net falls. Designs that only enforce STOP (c) change almost nothing because the agents already stop.
- The policy itself is the weak part: `src/policy_25.json` was fitted on release 5's option 2 dollars (cloned notes denied in full, cheap review), where escalating paid. Under score 4.0 on these seeds its escalations do not pay. A v4.0 policy refitted on score 4.0 and data 4.0 is the next step (the workflow RL loop), and only then is the question of binding it worth asking again.
