# Demand check (demand_check)

[Back to concepts](README.md) · [glossary](../glossary.md)

## What it means
What chart review attaches to a provider called upcoding: the payer's best estimate of the recovery, its [conservative estimate](conservative-estimate.md), the cost of a demand, whether the case is a [borderline case](borderline-case.md), and what to do next.

## Why it exists
It puts the economics of a demand next to the evidence, so the decision to pursue is made with the numbers in view.

## Example
Estimate $4,100, conservative estimate $1,350, demand cost $276: pursue. Estimate $1,400, conservative $150: borderline, request 30 more charts once.
