## What is by construction

- **No arm has a tool for fraud, waste and abuse.** A chart sample estimates the level difference. A not-rendered claim has no chart, so a request that includes one returns fewer charts and a `NO_RECORD` deficiency, which only triggers a follow-up round; it adds cost and no recovery. Impossible days and unbundling are in the claims tables (claims per provider per day; extra lines and modifier 25 in `claim_lines.csv`) and the detector view does not carry them. So the dollars they hold are 'available' and unreachable here.
- **Score 4.0 does not penalize missing records** (the Program Integrity Manual treats sampling units without records as overpayments; not modeled, issue #68). A ghost panel therefore costs the provider nothing in recovery; it only shrinks the sample.
- **The 'justified' label is derived at evaluation time** from dollars and is not a planted truth. Providers with a small positive net rate and a large panel can clear the dollar line: they are 'worth' pursuing only if a sample can show it, and the extrapolation gate (error rate of at least 15%) keeps the arms from demanding on a 3% net rate, so the arms rarely pursue them.
- **Data 4.2 is a different world from data 4.0** (a heavy-tailed rate, fraud kinds, a larger honest noise-driven dollar pool); scores here are not comparable with data 4.0's. W2.5, W4.0 and W4.1 were tuned on data 4.0; on data 4.2 they are references, not candidates.
- **Twenty seeds, one office draw each.** The spread between seeds is large (see the per-seed table); differences of a few hundred dollars per seed are inside it.
