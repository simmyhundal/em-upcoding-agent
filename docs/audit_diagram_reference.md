# Reference for the audit swimlane (from Simmy, 2026-10-27)

Starting point for the "Workflow in context" diagram (`docs/images/audit_workflow.svg`). Not copied into the repository (third-party figure); the structure we take from it:

- **Columns are phases**: before care, at care, coding and edits, claim goes out, payer intake, payer review, decision, pay or deny, appeal, patient and funding. Our diagram adds the audit stages after payment (audit, recovery, collections, appeals, vendor fees) and zooms into them.
- **Rows are participants**: patient, care team, billing office (provider), payer claims, payer clinical and appeals, others. Ours splits the payer row into SIU, coder, clinician and software product.
- **Legend**: step on every bill, step sometimes, step documented in a published study (the figure cites a JAMA 2018 study of a health-system billing office), denial and appeal path drawn in a different line style.
- **Honesty line**: the figure says which steps are documented and which are illustration. Ours does the same: sourced step, illustrated step, simulated step.
