# Release 2: which upcoders were missed, and why

[Back to the README](../README.md)

Generated by `python eval/analyze_misses_release2.py` from the stored runs and the answer key (free). Ten seeds (1515 to 2424), 78 planted upcoders on data 2.2, screen `eb_ratio` top 5%, workflow v2.2 and agent v3.2 (Opus 5.5, low effort). Descriptive: it reads the same ten seeds the results came from, so reasons below are hypotheses to test on fresh seeds, not tuned fixes.

## Summary

- **Precision is not the weak number; recall is.** Of the doctors each arm called upcoding, 91% (workflow) and 92% (agent) were upcoders. Recall is 27% and 15%.
- **The screen caps recall at 56%.** A top-5% list holds only about 11 doctors per seed, and 34 of the 78 upcoders sit just outside it (ranks 12 to 195; 21 of the 34 are at ranks 12 to 27). Neither arm asked for records on any of them. A wider list raises the ceiling (top 10%: 76%, top 15%: 85%) but sends more honest practices requests.
- **Cloned notes are the biggest hole.** 23 cloned-notes upcoders were screened; the workflow called 4 and the agent 0. Cloning makes the charts match the bill, so documentation 'supports billing' and the rules stop at inconclusive, even when the share is far above expected. For honest-notes upcoders that were screened the workflow called 17 of 21 and the agent 12 of 21.
- **Subtle upcoders are mostly never seen by the screen:** 15 of the 18 subtle ones are outside the top 5%. Their true rates are close to the 6-point line, which is the point of them.
- **The agent is more cautious than the workflow once it has records:** it asked for records on 41 upcoders and called 12; the workflow asked on 44 and called 21. Its reasons were not read case by case here (open question for a follow-up).
- **Everything above reads the same ten seeds the results came from.** These are hypotheses for fresh seeds, not changes to make and then call held out.
