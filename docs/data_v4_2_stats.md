# Data 4.2 development seeds: what the generator produced

[Back to the README](../README.md) · [data 4.2](data_v4_2.md)

Generated by `python eval/data_v42_stats.py`. Free. 100 development seeds built (1001 to 4664 step 37 where built), each 216 providers, no planted role. Rates are net (billed above minus below the justified level) on the live rendered claims the detector view keeps, the basis of score 4.0 (a phantom claim is justified at its billed level). Claim-level tables use the first 20 seeds. The pursuit cost used for the dollar threshold is $758 (30 charts, two rounds, one demand, triage, score 4.0's prices).
