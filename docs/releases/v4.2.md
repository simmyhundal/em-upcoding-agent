# v4.2

The workflow on data 4.2 (D4.2, no planted upcoder, fraud as well as upcoding) and score 4.2 (S4.2). Workflow v4.1 plus a **claims-data lane**: a provider who billed 30 or more visits on one service date gets a random sample of that day's claims requested, a visit the office has no record of counts as overpaid, and a demand goes out when the conservative recovery estimate covers its cost. Frozen as H28 before its confirm read ([docs/w42_freeze.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v4.2/docs/w42_freeze.md)); tested once on ten fresh seeds (4700 + 37j).

![The flow this version builds](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/main/docs/images/workflow.svg)

## What changed
1. **Data 4.2 and score 4.2.** No `role` in the answer key; a continuous true rate and true dollars per provider. Score 4.2: copy fee $0, records not provided counted as overpaid (PIM 8.4.5), stratified-sample recovery, abrasion by total true rate.
2. **The claims-data lane** (`src/fwa_lane.py`): volume screen, stratified sample of the flagged days' claims, break-even rule, up to four follow-ups when the office gives nothing usable.
3. Workflow v4.1 is unchanged underneath: list of 4, looser chart test, borderline second sample.

## Headline result
Ten test seeds: **+$375,031 in all (+$37,503 per seed)** against **+$17,713** for workflow v4.1 re-run on the same seeds: better on 10 of 10, one-sided exact p = 0.001. On the confirm pool (100 seeds, read once as declared): +$33,601 per seed over v4.1, 95 of 100 seeds. 94% of the recovery is the lane; it sent a demand to 30 of 36 providers with impossible-day billing and to no other provider.

## Known limits
- **The signal is clean by construction** (busiest honest day 26 visits, least busy impossible-days provider 33). The gain is what a reviewer does with a clean signal, not a forecast for real claims.
- Level upcoding is still barely reached (38 of 478 justified providers sent a demand).
- Office response behavior is simulated; the follow-up persistence result depends on it.
- Same data and score limits as v4.1; see [docs/reflection_v42_v52.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v4.2/docs/reflection_v42_v52.md).
