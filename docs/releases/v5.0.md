# v5.0

The agent on data 4.0 and score 4.0. It mirrors workflow v4.0 inside its tools. Evaluated once on the same ten fresh seeds (5555 to 6464; $1.14 spent).

![The flow this version builds on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/main/docs/images/agent.svg)

## What changed
1. **The `eb_upper_t` screen and rules 4.0** in the agent's tools, with ask advice refitted on score 4.0 inside the tool results.
2. **Advice to stop after the first sample, and the demand floor** (the agent may not call upcoding where the estimated recovery does not cover a demand).
3. **A code repair of the two writing defects** the rule judge scores (a headline figure the rationale never states, and a repeated sentence), instead of refusing the summary.
4. The binding rule from v3.5 stays.

## Headline result
Ten fresh seeds: **3 of 80 upcoders found, 0 honest providers accused, score 4.0 of -$7,812 in all (-$781 per seed)**, against -$11,612 for workflow v4.0 (+$380 per seed, better on 10 of 10 seeds, the same 3 upcoders found) and -$111,847 for the frozen v2.5. **Not above zero.** The frozen agent v3.5 was not run on these seeds.

Full tables: [docs/v4_v5_results.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v5.0/docs/v4_v5_results.md).

## Known limits
- The agent's gain over the workflow comes from lower staff cost, not more catches; ten seeds cannot confirm it.
- Parity with the workflow is near, not exact (the same providers asked on 8 of 8 development seeds, the same providers called on 6 of 8).
- Same data and score limits as v4.0.
