# v2.3

The workflow with cloned-notes detection and a wider list, on the same data 2.2. Evaluated once on ten fresh seeds with a pre-registered rule (issue 99).

![The flow this version builds on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/main/docs/images/how_it_works_v2.0.svg)

## What changed
1. **Rules 2.3.** Late-signature or audit-trail signs of cloned notes lean upcoding when the 99215 share is more than 3 times expected, even though the charts support the bill; a chart-based call must also beat the real peer rate by more than chance.
2. **Screen `eb_ratio_t`:** eb_ratio with the lab or test line on the claim as a covariate.
3. **Wider list, two tiers:** top 10%; the first 5% get the full policy, the rest one request unless the notes show cloning.
4. Judge j3.3 for these summaries ([judge_release3.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v2.3/docs/judge_release3.md)).

## Headline result
Ten fresh seeds (2525 to 3434): **37 of 79 upcoders found (47% recall), 1 honest accused (97% precision)**, 150 honest practices asked for charts, against **24 of 79 upcoders found (30% recall), 2 honest accused (92% precision)**, 64 honest practices asked for charts for v2.2. Workflow v2.3 against v2.2: 21 upcoders found only by v2.3, 8 only by v2.2; p = 0.049 against the pre-registered 0.025, so **not significant by the rule** (exact McNemar 0.024 beside it).

Full tables: [docs/release3_results.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v2.3/docs/release3_results.md) and [docs/eval61.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v2.3/docs/eval61.md).

## Known limits
- The wider list asks 150 honest practices for charts against 64 for v2.2 (agent v3.3: 79).
- On the development seeds it found 38 of 78 (in sample); 37 of 79 held out.
- Most remaining misses are upcoders the screen never lists.
