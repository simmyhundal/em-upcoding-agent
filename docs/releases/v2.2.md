# v2.2

The workflow on harder data (data 2.2): honest doctors miscode too, practices bill through groups, and the extract can have defects. Evaluated with agent v3.2 on ten seeds.

![The flow this version builds on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/main/docs/images/how_it_works_v2.0.svg)

## What changed
1. **Data 2.2.** Honest coding noise (about 8% of honest visits miscoded, half upward), group billing, level-changing corrections, optional diagnosis truncation and injected data defects; an upcoder is a provider whose true rate is above the honest 95th percentile and at least 6 points above the honest mean ([data_v2_2.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v2.2/docs/data_v2_2.md)).
2. **Screen.** `eb_ratio` (empirical-Bayes shrinkage of the ratio to expected), top 5% of the panel.
3. **Rules 2.2.** A peer-rate cap for noisy honest peers, and the cases the model always answered inconclusive are decided by rule: about $0.01 a seed, no LLM judge.
4. **Data-quality pre-flight** on the delivered extract, with a hold on findings affected by a data problem; deterministic rule judge j3.2.

## Headline result
Ten seeds (1515 to 2424): **21 of 78 upcoders found (27% recall), 2 honest accused (91% precision)**, 66 honest practices asked for charts. Agent v3.2 on the same seeds found fewer. The comparison was an **unplanned ten-seed look** (the pre-registered looks were at 22 and 44 seeds), so it carries no significance claim: 10 upcoders found only by the workflow against 1 only by the agent (exact McNemar p = 0.012; with a design effect for seeds as clusters p = 0.027).

Full tables: [docs/release2_results.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v2.2/docs/release2_results.md) and [docs/eval61.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v2.2/docs/eval61.md).

## Known limits
- Misses most upcoders: the top-5% list held only 44 of 78 upcoders, and cloned-notes upcoders were the largest hole (4 of 23 screened ones found).
- Seeds 1515 to 2424 became development data afterwards.
- Notes are generated, so cloned-notes signs are probably easier than real ones.
