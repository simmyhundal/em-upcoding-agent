# v4.0

The workflow on data 4.0 and score 4.0 (the v4/v5 line). Frozen after a reinforcement-learning loop on 100 development seeds (issue 131); evaluated once on ten fresh seeds (5555 to 6464).

![The flow this version builds on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/main/docs/images/workflow.svg)

## What changed
1. **Data 4.0.** Every provider has a true upcoding rate; the 8 upcoders per dataset each have a habit (systematic, opportunistic or template-driven). Data 2.x stays reproducible.
2. **Score 4.0.** Dollars recovered, minus staff-hours cost, minus provider abrasion (every parameter is documented with its source or marked as an assumption in `docs/score_40.md`).
3. **Screen `eb_upper_t`:** the shrunk share of visits billed 99214 or 99215, with the lab or test line, top 10% of providers.
4. **Rules 4.0, a refitted ask-or-skip, no escalation, and a demand floor** (a demand only when the estimated recovery covers its staff cost). Deterministic and API-free.

## Headline result
Ten fresh seeds (5555 to 6464, 80 upcoders): **3 of 80 upcoders found, 0 honest providers accused, score 4.0 of -$11,612 in all (-$1,161 per seed)**, against 1 of 80 found, 3 honest accused and -$111,847 for the frozen v2.5 on the same seeds (better on 10 of 10 seeds). **Not above zero.** On the 100 development seeds it scored +$549 per seed (77 of 800 found), so part of that was fitted to the development pool.

Full tables: [docs/v4_v5_results.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v4.0/docs/v4_v5_results.md) and [docs/w_loop_v4.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v4.0/docs/w_loop_v4.md).

## Known limits
- Synthetic data; the generator plants the lab-line link that `eb_upper_t` uses (issue 144).
- Staff-minute, abrasion and sample-gate parameters are assumptions; dollars per provider are a Medicare-sized slice (issue 145).
- Recall is 4% on fresh seeds; the opportunistic habit is found least often (issue 134).
- Two steps were not confirmed batch by batch under the loop's own protocol (rules 4.0 and the refitted ask model); see `docs/w_loop_v4.md`.
