# v2.5

The workflow with ask-or-skip and stop-or-escalate both learned on net dollars, deterministic and API-free, on data 2.2. Evaluated once on ten fresh seeds with a pre-registered rule (issue 109).

![The flow this version builds on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/main/docs/images/workflow.svg)

## What changed
1. **Net dollars are the score.** The overpayment a records sample supports (90% lower bound, cloned notes denied in full) minus $90 per round, $43.84 per round to an honest provider, $158 per provider reviewed and $2,000 per honest provider accused (`eval/dollar_reward.py`).
2. **Learned ask-or-skip and stop-or-escalate** fitted on that score over 30 development seeds (`src/policy_25.json`).
3. **No API calls.** Where the rules are silent the summary is code-written and inconclusive; on the ten test seeds the results are identical with and without the model.

## Headline result
Ten fresh seeds (4545 to 5454): **37 of 79 upcoders found (47% recall), 3 honest accused (92% precision), $31,888 net per seed**. On the same seeds workflow v2.4 earned $29,569 and v2.3 $24,086. Recall did not move: the gain is lower cost and fewer false accusations (docs/release5_results.md).

Full tables: [docs/release5_results.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v2.5/docs/release5_results.md) and [docs/release5_dollar_baseline.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v2.5/docs/release5_dollar_baseline.md).

## Known limits
- Recall stays under half; dollars rank versions at simulated scale and rest on the optimistic recovery model and an assumed $2,000 per false accusation.
- The policy was fitted on 30 development seeds; ten test seeds with about eight upcoders each.
