# v2.0

The workflow on plan-shaped data: charts come only by request, code writes the facts and settles clear cases, a model handles the rest.

![Statistics screen, code asks for charts and settles the clear cases, AI handles the rest. Humans review.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/main/docs/images/how_it_works_v2.0.svg)

## How it works
1. **Claims data.** Plan-shaped synthetic data: members, claim lines, denials. Conditions are derived from the claims.
2. **Statistical screen.** Flags doctors who bill the top code more than their patients explain: suspicion 5 or higher.
3. **Records request.** Asks each flagged doctor's office for charts. Some come late, incomplete or not at all.
4. **Chart rules.** Code checks whether the charts support the level billed and decides the clear-cut cases.
5. **AI interpretation.** Where the rules are silent, a model reads a compact evidence card and gives an advisory leaning.
6. **Checks.** A citation check and a number check reject anything unsupported by the evidence.
7. **Case pages and packets.** Draft case pages and recovery packets for the doctors called upcoding.
8. **People review.** SIU reviewers read the drafts and decide. Nothing is sent automatically.

## Headline result
Two reserved seeds: 6 of 16 upcoders found, 0 honest accused; 17 honest practices asked for charts.

Numbers are not comparable across versions (different data and inputs). Full table: [docs/results.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/main/docs/results.md).

## Known limits
- Synthetic data from one generator; 8 upcoders per run.
- Cloned notes defeat the chart rules and the model.
- At 0.5% to 1% upcoders the AI step accuses many honest doctors.
- v2 schemas not yet reviewed by someone who works with 837/834/835 data.
