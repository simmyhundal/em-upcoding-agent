# v2.4

The workflow with a learned rule for when to stop asking and a ratio gate on the second tier, on data 2.2. Evaluated once on ten fresh seeds with a pre-registered rule (issue 108).

![The flow this version builds on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/main/docs/images/workflow.svg)

## What changed
1. **Learned stop-or-escalate.** After a provider's first sample (30 random charts and a follow-up), a fitted formula picks stop, a targeted request, the audit trail, or both (`src/policy_24.json`, fitted offline on 20 development seeds).
2. **Ratio gate.** Beyond the first 5% of the list, a provider is asked only when its 99215 share is at least 2.5 times expected.
3. **Default.** The variant with both (GL) was chosen by a rule fixed in advance; the variants with one of them (G, L) are kept for comparison.

## Headline result
Ten fresh seeds (3535 to 4444): **49 of 80 upcoders found (61% recall), 2 honest accused (96% precision), $32,245 net per seed**. Against workflow v2.3 on the same seeds (46 of 80 found, 6 honest accused, $26,399 net per seed), GL sent about 11 fewer rounds per seed to honest providers; the difference in upcoders found was not significant (docs/release4_results.md). Release 4 was scored in points at the time; the dollar score was applied afterwards.

Full tables: [docs/release4_results.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v2.4/docs/release4_results.md) and [docs/release5_dollar_baseline.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v2.4/docs/release5_dollar_baseline.md).
