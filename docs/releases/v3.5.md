# v3.5

The agent with the learned advice inside its tool results, on data 2.2. Evaluated once on the release 5 fresh seeds (issue 109).

![The flow this version builds on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/main/docs/images/agent.svg)

## What changed
1. **Advice inside tool results** (`request_records`, `follow_up_records`, `request_audit_trail`, and an `ask_advice` column in the screen), from the same policy file as workflow v2.5; use of the advice is logged.
2. **A narrow binding rule.** An upcoding call by the rules that the chart rule also supports cannot be overridden without a stated reason naming a fact the rules cannot see.
3. **Practice status every turn:** charts back, documented 99215 claims, rounds sent and their dollar cost.

## Headline result
Ten fresh seeds (4545 to 5454): **36 of 79 upcoders found (46% recall), 1 honest accused (97% precision), $32,159 net per seed**, against 37 of 79 upcoders found (47% recall), 3 honest accused (92% precision), $31,888 net per seed for workflow v2.5; a paired gap of $270 per seed (range -$29,015 to +$17,537), **not a confirmed difference**. The agent followed the advice on 79% of 155 actions and overrode a binding call twice, correctly both times. Ten runs cost $2.74.

Full tables: [docs/release5_results.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v3.5/docs/release5_results.md) and [docs/release5_dollar_baseline.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v3.5/docs/release5_dollar_baseline.md).

## Known limits
- The agent's summaries score lower on the free rule judge (4.54 against 4.89 for the workflow): its own headlines quote figures the rationale does not state, and it repeats sentences. A wording issue, not weaker evidence; fixing it is issue 116.
- Recall under half; same caveats as v2.5.
