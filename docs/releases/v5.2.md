# v5.2

The agent on data 4.2 (D4.2) and score 4.2 (S4.2), mirroring workflow v4.2 inside its tools. Run once on the ten test seeds (4700 + 37j; **$1.781 for ten runs**, below the $2.5 estimate and the $4 release cap).

![The flow this version builds on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/main/docs/images/agent.svg)

## What changed
1. **Three tools for the claims-data lane:** `volume_screen` (providers who billed 30 or more visits on one day), `request_stratum` (a random sample of the claims on those days, requested from the office, with a conservative estimate that counts a visit the office has no record of as overpaid) and `send_demand` (refused when the estimate does not cover a demand).
2. **Office 4.2:** a claim for a visit that never happened gets no chart; the office reports no record of it.
3. Agent v5.0, v5.1 and v3.x are unchanged.

## Headline result
Ten test seeds: **+$372,114 in all (+$37,211 per seed)**, 10 of 10 seeds above zero, 30 of 36 providers with impossible-day billing demanded from and no other. Against workflow v4.2 on the same seeds: −$292 per seed, better on 2 of 10, p = 0.94 (not distinguishable; both run the same lane code). Against workflow v4.1: +$35,440 per seed, 10 of 10. Agent v5.1 was not re-run.

A scripted agent that follows the advice matched the workflow's asks, calls, lane providers and lane demands on 20 of 20 development seeds and its score within 1% on 18 of 20 ([docs/agent_v52.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v5.2/docs/agent_v52.md)).

Full tables: [docs/v42_test_result.md](https://github.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/blob/v5.2/docs/v42_test_result.md).

## Known limits
- One run per seed, ten seeds; the model followed the advice, so the result says little about an agent finding the lane unaided; run-to-run noise is unmeasured.
- The signal is clean by construction; level upcoding is still barely reached.
