# v4.1

The workflow on data 4.0 (D4.0), still scored by score 4.0 (S4.0), improved by the v4.1 development loop (issue 132; eleven hypotheses tried, H13 to H23). Frozen as H23 at N = 4 before its test (docs/w41_freeze.md) and run once on the ten test seeds of v4.0 (5555 to 6464, a second use).

![The flow this version builds on](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simmyhundal/em-upcoding-agent/main/docs/images/workflow.svg)

## What changed
1. **A list of 4 providers** from the `eb_upper_t` ranking, so only 4 are summarized and triaged (triage is charged per listed provider; a mechanical saving of about $236 per seed that the control isolates).
2. **A looser chart test** (at least 5 documented 99214 and 99215 charts, at least 10% unsupported, alpha 0.20); the demand floor still blocks weak calls.
3. **One near-miss extension:** a second random request of 30 charts for a rules-called provider whose point estimate is at least twice the cost of a demand but whose 90% lower bound is below it.

## Headline result
Ten test seeds (80 planted upcoders): **9 found, 0 honest providers called, score 4.0 of +$10,031 in all (+$1,003 per seed)**, against 3 found and −$11,612 for v4.0: better on 10 of 10 seeds, one-sided exact p = 0.001, and +$1,782 per seed over the control (v4.0 with the same 4-provider list; p = 0.031). Against zero p = 0.27: **not distinguishable from zero.** One seed (5959, +$10,549) carries the total; without it v4.1 averages about −$58 per seed against −$1,206 for v4.0.

On the 60 hold-out development seeds v4.1 led v4.0 by about $677 per seed, of which $286 is the shorter list and $392 the rules and extension (p = 0.0087 against the control).

## Known limits
- Ten seeds, a second look at seeds whose W4.0 result was known during design (the second fresh pool, 6565 to 7474, is untouched).
- Honest providers are rarely asked, so zero called is weak evidence that the loosened rules are safe.
- Synthetic data; the lab-line link the screen uses is planted by the generator; staff-time and dollar scales are assumptions.
- Opportunistic upcoders are still not reached (chart review finds about a quarter of their true overpayment).
