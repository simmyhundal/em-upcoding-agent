## Reading

- **T2.5 against B:** 70 against 74 upcoders found, 238 against 307 honest practices asked (22% fewer), 700 against 853 rounds on honest practices, honest doctors called 4 against 4.
- **The pre-registered rule is conservative on purpose.** Harder gates (T3, T3.5, A3) ask many fewer honest practices per upcoder found (2.5 to 2.8 against 4.2), but each loses more than a fifth of B's upcoders: the cloned-notes upcoders whose ratio sits between 2.5 and 3.5 are the ones given up. Whether that trade is worth it is a judgement, not a measurement.

## Issue 104: high-acuity calls and the late-signature sign

Rules 2.3 called 50 listed providers a high-acuity panel over the 20 development seeds: {'normal': 39, 'hard negative': 6, 'upcoder (cloned notes)': 5}. Only 2 of them have a late-signature rate of 12% or more over at least 8 notes: ['upcoder (cloned notes)', 'normal']. True high-acuity panels (hard negatives, 12 with at least 8 notes) have late rates of at most 10%, so a late-signature override would not hit them. But the override would change one cloned-notes upcoder and one honest doctor, no more, and four of the five cloned-notes upcoders called high-acuity have late rates below the line. **Not built:** at most one more upcoder for one more wrong flip is not a reason for a rule; the issue stays open for the fresh seeds.
