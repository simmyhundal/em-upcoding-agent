# Case summary usefulness rubric (1-5)

Score each case summary on whether an SIU investigator could act on it. Score from the summary and the
evidence packet only; do not look at the answer key first.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Clear headline; explains billed level vs patient complexity with specific numbers; cites claims that really support each point; names concrete next steps (for example, request documentation for the cited visits); cautious language; flags what is missing |
| 4 | Accurate and grounded; one weakness (a vague next step, a missing number, or slightly thin evidence) |
| 3 | Mostly accurate but generic; an investigator would have to redo some of the analysis |
| 2 | Partly wrong or poorly grounded (claims not supported by the cited claims, or numbers misread) |
| 1 | Misleading, fabricated, or unusable |

Notes
- A rejected summary (failed citation check) scores 1 regardless of text quality.
- Score the summary against the packet, not against whether the provider is truly an upcoder.
- Target: average of 4 or higher across the flagged providers.
